Every possible state of a single qubit lives on the surface of this sphere.

The north pole is |0⟩ — a definite 0.
Measure it, you always get 0.
The south pole is |1⟩ — always gives you 1.

The latitude tells you the probabilities.
Near the north pole — mostly 0, a little chance of 1.
On the equator — exactly 50/50.

The longitude is phase — a hidden degree of freedom you can't see by measuring alone.
But it matters enormously when qubits interact with each other.

The Bloch sphere is the map.
Every quantum gate is a rotation on this sphere.
