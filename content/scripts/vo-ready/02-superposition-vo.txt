Schrödinger's cat is the worst explanation for superposition.
Here's what it actually is.

A qubit in superposition has a definite quantum state —
but that state doesn't tell you if you'll get 0 or 1 when you measure.
It tells you the probability of each.
Equal superposition means 50/50.
Not both at once — a weighted probability distribution.

When you measure, the qubit picks one.
Randomly, weighted by those probabilities.
The state collapses to a definite 0 or 1 — and the superposition is gone.
You only get one shot.

Superposition is a real physical state — not a metaphor, not a trick.
The whole Bloch sphere is a map of every possible qubit state.
