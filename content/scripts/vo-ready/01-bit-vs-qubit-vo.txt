Your laptop stores everything as 0 or 1.
A qubit can be something in between — and that changes everything.

A classical bit is a switch. It's either off or on.
0 or 1. That's it.
Every photo, message, and video on your phone is just billions of these switches.

A qubit lives on a sphere.
The north pole is 0, the south pole is 1 —
but the vector can point anywhere in between.
That in-between state is called superposition.

Superposition isn't magic — it's a real physical state, like a coin mid-spin.
