## Script

```
[0:00–0:03] HOOK
[VISUAL: Photo of Schrödinger's cat meme, then X through it]
[VO] "Schrödinger's cat is the worst explanation
     for superposition. Here's what it actually is."

[0:03–0:25] THE REAL EXPLANATION
[VISUAL: Bloch sphere — vector on equator, θ and φ labels]
[VO] "A qubit in superposition has a definite quantum state —
     but that state doesn't tell you if you'll get 0 or 1
     when you measure. It tells you the *probability* of each.
     Equal superposition means 50/50. Not both at once —
     a weighted probability distribution."

[0:25–0:45] MEASUREMENT COLLAPSE
[VISUAL: Animate — equator state, click Measure button,
 vector snaps to north pole]
[VO] "When you measure, the qubit picks one. Randomly,
     weighted by those probabilities. The state collapses
     to a definite 0 or 1 — and the superposition is gone.
     You only get one shot."

[0:45–0:58] TAKEAWAY + TEASE
[VISUAL: Sphere with equator highlighted]
[VO] "Superposition is a real physical state — not a metaphor,
     not a trick. The whole Bloch sphere is a map of
     every possible qubit state. Next video: a full tour."
```

---

## Production Notes

- The Schrödinger's cat meme open is intentionally provocative — it earns credibility by subverting the cliché
- Emphasize *probability distribution*, not "both at once" — this is the most common misconception to correct
- The Measure interaction is the money shot: show the vector physically snap to a pole. Record a few takes to get a clean collapse
- "You only get one shot" sets up the no-cloning theorem conceptually without naming it
