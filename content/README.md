# Quantum Computing 101 — YouTube Shorts Series

Educational short-form video series teaching quantum computing concepts at a 101 level. Each video is ≤60 seconds, vertical (9:16), with a hook in the first 3 seconds and one clear takeaway.

## The Simulator as a Visual Asset

The [Bloch sphere simulator](../index.html) is the primary visual for most videos — screen-recorded as gates are applied and states are manipulated. This gives the series a consistent visual identity and showcases the tool.

## Toolchain

Descript — handles voiceover (AI voice), screen recording, editing, captions, and 9:16 export in one tool. No self-recorded voiceover.
